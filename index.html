GUMGO is a vinyl-only DJ and selector. The name means 'black cat' in Korean.

Her sound is textural and groove-driven, rooted in late 90s and early 2000s electronics encountered as a child, and shaped by janggu rhythms she's been immersed in since a young age. Her sets move through minimal, electro, and anything heavy.

She has played underground clubs and radio platforms across Asia and Europe, and is currently setting her sights on a debut release in 2027.
