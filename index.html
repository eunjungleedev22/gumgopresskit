    2027Debut EP
    1. I play for the room, not for myself. A set is communication, not self-expression. Less a performance than a release.
    2. I learned time on the janggu, in cycles. A set climbs to its peak and comes back down.
    3. Subtraction first. Bass and percussion carry the weight; everything else has to earn its place.
    4. Material and repetition. The record is the material, and repetition is how it opens.
    5. Underground is an attitude, not a room size.

    Kit

    words

    GUMGO is a Korean DJ and vinyl curator based in Barcelona, digging the record shops of every city she plays. Trained on the janggu from age nine, she plays dub techno, electro and minimal built on bass and percussion. Featured in Mixmag Asia’s Artists Exciting Us 2026; sets for HÖR Berlin and Oslated. Debut EP due 2027.
