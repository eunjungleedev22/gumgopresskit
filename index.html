About

GUMGO is a Seoul-based DJ and producer carving space at the intersection of raw club culture and meticulously crafted electronic sound.

Drawing from techno, industrial, and experimental club music, GUMGO's sets are precision-engineered journeys — each transition a deliberate statement, each track selection a story beat. Residencies across Seoul's underground circuit have cemented a reputation for sets that never repeat and dancefloors that remember.

Original productions explore texture over melody: percussive structures that breathe, basslines that converse with silence, and an unflinching commitment to dynamic range. Available for bookings globally.
